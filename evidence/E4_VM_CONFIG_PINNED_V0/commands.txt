# E4 — Unshackled VM: pinned rotation-width / growth config, on Linux.
# Executed 2026-10-14 on the Linux host. vendor/Unshackled.c untouched; nothing
# outside evidence/E4_VM_CONFIG_PINNED_V0/ was written.

## Why
`vendor/Unshackled.c` draws its rotation-width policy from `srand(time(NULL))`
at startup: rotwidth = 10+rand()%6, growth_slack = rand()%6,
growth_step = 4+rand()%9, growth_prob (biased draw), det_growth = rand()%2.
So the same `.mu` executes a DIFFERENT NUMBER OF STEPS on every run, and
E3's `unsh_counted` binary was built in /tmp and is not preserved. This
experiment pins the config and re-measures.

## The patch (see patch_unsh.py, unsh_pinned.c)
1. `#include <sys/time.h>` (missing; `gettimeofday` was implicitly declared).
2. Config read from the environment, defaulting to the original random draw:
   UNSH_SEED, UNSH_ROTWIDTH, UNSH_GROWTH_SLACK, UNSH_GROWTH_STEP,
   UNSH_DET_GROWTH, UNSH_GROWTH_PROB.
3. A step counter plus one report line at `hlt`:
   UNSH_PIN STEPS <n> WALL <s>s SPS <n> cfg{...} final{rotwidth=.. max_wordwidth=..}
No change to Malbolge semantics.
