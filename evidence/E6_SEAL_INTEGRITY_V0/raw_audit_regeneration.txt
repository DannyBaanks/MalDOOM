## interpretation
seal_audit.py globs evidence/*/SHA256SUMS.txt, so writing this directory's own
manifest added a 33rd manifest with 10 entries. Every one of those files is
untracked and its worktree matches its seal, so the only movement is group 6
(exact / UNTRACKED): 24 -> 34. No pre-existing row changed category, and the 32
repairable files, the 103 CRLF seals and the 99 byte-exact rows are identical in
both runs.

The sealed raw_audit.tsv is kept as the capture that produced the findings; this
delta is appended rather than overwritten (AGENTS.md 5.10: never rewrite raw
evidence, append corrections). Caveat, stated plainly: the same mechanism will add
this control file to the fresh run on the next re-run, and any future manifest
under evidence/ moves these two numbers again. That self-reference is precisely the
defect documented for the 7 M0_*/SHA256SUMS.txt files, so the honest convention here
is: raw_audit.tsv is a dated capture, not a live count.
